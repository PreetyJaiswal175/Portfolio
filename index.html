Research Experience

M.Tech Thesis · OngoingGuide: Dr. Sarosh Alam Ghausi

Physics-Guided Climate Modeling and Spatio-Temporal Analytics

The thesis examines why India's long-term warming rate is about half the global average, by comparing temperature and surface-radiation trends on rainy and non-rainy days.

  • Built a data pipeline combining 70+ years (1951–2021) of IMD gridded observations across 4,434 grid points with ERA5 reanalysis and NASA CERES radiative-flux data at 1° × 1° resolution.
  • Computed temperature anomalies using 11-day moving climatological baselines and estimated trends with OLS regression and Mann-Kendall analysis.
  • Evaluated a physics-constrained thermodynamic maximum power model that reconstructs surface temperature with R² > 0.93 against station observations.
